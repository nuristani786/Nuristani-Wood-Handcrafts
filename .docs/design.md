# Design Parameters — Nuristani Wood Handcrafts

## Colors
- bg-primary: #1d140d (walnut dark)
- bg-panel: #2b1d14
- accent-gold: #c99a4b | hover: #e0bd7e
- accent-pine: #3e5c45 | hover: #5b7d62
- text-cream: #f1e9da
- text-muted: #cdbfa8
- border-gold: rgba(201,154,75,0.25)

## Typography
- FA headings (logo, h1, h2): Noto Nastaliq Urdu 600-700, line-height: 2
- FA body: Vazirmatn 300-400
- EN headings + numbers: Playfair Display 500-900
- EN caption italic: Cormorant Garamond

## Style
- Luxury, calm, generous whitespace
- No gradients, no flashy animations
- Product images: large, bordered with thin translucent gold border (thicker on hover)
- Nuristani geometric divider: zigzag triangular SVG pattern in translucent gold
- Rounded corners: 4px (slight, artisan feel)
